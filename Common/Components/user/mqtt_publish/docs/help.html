MQTT Publish Misc ↓ Params ↓ Ports ↓

The MQTT Publisher will publish messages from a message flow to a topic on an MQ Telemetry Transport (MQTT) server.

It will publish with a Quality Of Service (QOS) level, which is described by an integer. It can have 3 values. At Least Once (0), At Most Once (1) and Exactly Once (2).
