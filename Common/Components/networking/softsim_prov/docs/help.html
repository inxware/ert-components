SoftSIM Provisioning Misc ↓ Params ↓ Ports ↓

Loads a SoftSIM subscription profile into the modem and reports whether one is present.

A SoftSIM is a subscription implemented entirely in software, with no physical card. This block installs the profile; selecting SoftSIM as the active type afterwards is the SIM Manager's job.

The profile blob carries the subscription's secret keys. It is never logged by this block, and it should not be routed through ports or storage that end up in a log, a debug console, or an over-the-air message.

Check is always safe and always answers; it is the right way to find out whether provisioning is needed before sending a blob anywhere.
