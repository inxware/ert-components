Cell Power Manager Misc ↓ Params ↓ Ports ↓

Requests PSM and eDRX power saving, and reports what the network actually granted.

Power saving on cellular is negotiated, never simply set. The device asks; the network grants what it chooses, which is frequently different from and worse than the request, and sometimes nothing at all.

That is why every output here is named 'granted'. Always read back after applying - an app that assumes it got the timers it asked for will size its wake schedule wrongly and either drain the battery or miss downlink windows.

ADone means the request was accepted by the modem, not that the network agreed to it.

Out Ev.

RDone Fires once the granted values have been refreshed.
