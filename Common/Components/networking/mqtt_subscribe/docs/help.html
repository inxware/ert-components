MQTT Subscribe Misc ↓ Params ↓ Ports ↓

The MQTT Subscribe function block connects to an MQTT broker to receive messages published to a specific topic. It supports subscribing and unsubscribing from topics at a specified Quality of Service (QoS) level, which determines the reliability of message delivery. The block can operate at three QoS levels: At Most Once (0), At Least Once (1), and Exactly Once (2). Upon receiving a message, the function block outputs the data and its size to the corresponding ports.
