Accelerometer Misc ↓ Params ↓ Ports ↓

Accelerometer reads the onboard 3-axis accelerometer on Strato Pi Max boards.

Acceleration values are reported in milli-g (mg), where 1000 mg = 1 g = 9.81 m/s². The Z axis reads approximately +1000 mg when the board is horizontal and stationary. Poll read at your required sample rate — the board samples at its own hardware rate and the sysfs attribute returns the most recent value.

enable powers on the accelerometer IC (if it has a power-down mode). disable powers it down. If your application reads infrequently, disable between reads to reduce power consumption.
